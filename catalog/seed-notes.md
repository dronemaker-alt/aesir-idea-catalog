# Seed Notes — 2026-09

## IDEA-001 — OpenArm
**Why saved:** Open-source humanoid/robot arm platform may provide reusable actuator, reducer, encoder, CAN/control and ROS patterns without requiring Æsir to develop a humanoid.

## IDEA-002 — XY gantry
**Why saved:** Compact enclosed extrusion/linear-motion architecture maps directly onto Cali-Eye automated inspection: camera, microscope, line laser and interchangeable sensor/tool heads.

## IDEA-003 — ESP32 battery dashboard
**Why saved:** A common battery telemetry node could serve UAV, UGV, USV and bench systems with a shared local web UI.

## IDEA-004 — Mobile carrier
**Why saved:** Large autonomous carrier deploying smaller specialized vehicles parallels the integrated search/recovery architecture.

## IDEA-005/006 — Plastic recycling
**Why saved:** Adds a material-recovery path to the proposed e-waste/research/outreach program; failed prints and suitable thermoplastics become feedstock instead of waste.

## IDEA-007 — Articulated Horus head
**Why saved:** Mechanism and presentation inspiration for a physical Cali/lab interface and the earlier Mechanicus/servitor-skull concept.

## IDEA-008 — Modular drone stack
**Why saved:** Clean service access and modular electronics reinforce the existing ÆSIR design philosophy: primary structure remains distinct from replaceable avionics/panels.

## IDEA-009 — ICOM battery upgrade
**Why saved:** Example of retaining useful professional hardware while modernizing obsolete energy storage.

## IDEA-010 — Fiber carrier link
**Why saved:** Carrier maintains radio/network connectivity while a deployed vehicle uses fiber for the final segment; useful architecture to study for contested or difficult RF environments.

## IDEA-011 — Sonar logging
**Why saved:** Sonar data collection and mapping can feed the USV/ROV side of integrated search, with later AI-assisted interpretation.

## IDEA-012 — CAN telemetry display
**Why saved:** Reusable diagnostic/display node could expose distributed vehicle health and subsystem data across multiple platforms.

## IDEA-013 — HUMS-like monitoring
**Why saved:** Familiar aircraft HUMS philosophy applied to robots: trend subsystem health rather than waiting for hard failures.

## IDEA-014 — BeagleBone
**Why saved:** Linux SBC with strong hardware I/O sits between microcontrollers and larger Pi-class companion computers.

## IDEA-015 — Encoders
**Why saved:** Encoder feedback resolved the previously identified mechanism/indexing problem; directly applicable to Cali-Eye rotary positioning.

## IDEA-016 — U-channel spine
**Why saved:** Upper/lower structural datums with printed stations and replaceable skins give a scalable, serviceable construction language across ÆSIR vehicles.

## IDEA-017 — Sonobot 5
**Why saved:** Commercial validation of a compact, portable twin-hull survey USV closely aligned with FISK. Mine its deployment, propulsion, battery, sonar and packaging choices while keeping ÆSIR's implementation open, modular, lower-cost and integrated with UAV/ROV/NOMAD assets.


## IDEA-018 — Project Quiver
**Why saved:** Open-source ~25 kg-class heavy-lift UAV reference with modular/hot-swappable payload interfaces and unusually disciplined CAD/BOM/manufacturing documentation. Mine both the aircraft architecture and the repository/process structure for Sleipnir/Jotun/ÆSIR development; compare against the frozen ÆSIR structural baseline before adopting changes.
